Don’t just learn tech — use it. An after-school tech academy helping students build real-world digital skills through hands-on projects.

Welcome to Triple T — Turn to Tech

TurnToTech Academy is currently launching its after-school programs at Besançon Baabdat School, where students join weekly sessions to explore, build, and grow their technology skills. Our academy operates within the school environment, offering structured after-school programs designed to take students beyond the classroom and into real-world tech application..

Our Mission:

We don’t just teach technology — we guide students through a complete learning journey:
1- Start with foundational digital skills.
2- Progress to building real projects (games, websites, simple apps)
3- Develop problem-solving and critical thinking.
4- Prepare for advanced learning, competitions, and future tech paths.
Our goal is to help students move from users of technology to creators of technology. At the same time, we support teachers with training and ready-to-use strategies to confidently integrate technology into their classrooms. Our goal is simple: to bring measurable impact to your school by improving student engagement, strengthening digital competencies, and preparing learners for the demands of the modern world.
