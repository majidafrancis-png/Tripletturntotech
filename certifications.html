Skills & Recognition

Beyond Grades: Proof of Competency

At Triple T Academy, we don't just hand out papers. We track every student's journey through a rigorous board of competencies and prepare them for the adrenaline of live tech competitions.

How We Measure Growth

A New Kind of Assessment

The Competency Board

Every student follows a structured roadmap. We assess them based on "Can-Do" statements rather than just test scores. This ensures they master the physical and digital skills needed for the future.

  • Circuit Design & Troubleshooting
  • Logic & Algorithmic Thinking
  • Hardware-Software Integration
  • Collaborative Project Management

Real-World Validation

Instead of traditional exams, our students prove their skills by building functional prototypes. Each milestone completed is recorded in their personal digital portfolio, ready to be showcased to future schools or employers.
