Our Story

WHO ARE WE?

Triple T Academy was founded by Ms. Majida Francis Antonios, a Computer Instructor, EdTech trainer, and digital learning specialist with more than 20 years of experience in education and technology. Passionate about empowering youth through technology, she works to make digital skills, coding, AI, and innovation accessible to all learners.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional education and the rapidly evolving world of technology by creating engaging, hands-on learning experiences for students of all ages.

Through expertise in educational technology, coding, robotics, AI, and digital creativity, we aim to inspire the next generation of innovators, problem-solvers, and future tech leaders.
